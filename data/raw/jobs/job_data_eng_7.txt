Job Title: Analytics Engineer
Company: MetricFlow

Job Description:
We are seeking an Analytics Engineer to sit at the intersection of data engineering and business intelligence. You will build the data models and transformation layers that power company-wide analytics and reporting.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and build clean, reliable data models in our data warehouse using dbt.
- Write well-documented SQL transformations following dimensional modelling principles.
- Collaborate with analysts to define metrics, dimensions, and KPIs.
- Maintain data quality tests and documentation within dbt.
- Build and maintain BI dashboards in Looker, Metabase, or Tableau.
- Manage ingestion pipelines using tools like Fivetran or Airbyte.

Requirements:
- Strong SQL skills and understanding of data modelling concepts.
- Experience with data warehouses (BigQuery, Redshift, or Snowflake).
- Analytical mindset with ability to translate business questions into data models.
- Familiarity with version control (Git).

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with dbt (data build tool) for transformation.
- Knowledge of Python for data pipeline scripting.
- Familiarity with Apache Airflow for orchestration.
- Understanding of batch processing and stream processing trade-offs.
