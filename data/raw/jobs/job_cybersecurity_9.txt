Job Title: Vulnerability Management Analyst
Company: Fortify Digital

Job Description:
We are looking for a Vulnerability Management Analyst to identify, assess, and track security vulnerabilities across our technology estate. You will manage the full vulnerability lifecycle from discovery to remediation verification.

Key Responsibilities:
- Run regular vulnerability scans using Tenable Nessus and Qualys.
- Prioritise vulnerabilities using CVSS scores and business risk context.
- Work with system owners to track and drive remediation activities.
- Produce executive and technical vulnerability reports and trend dashboards.
- Maintain the organisation's patch management programme.
- Conduct risk assessments in line with ISO 27001 and POPIA compliance requirements.

Requirements:
- Understanding of vulnerability scoring systems (CVSS v3.x).
- Knowledge of common vulnerability types across network, OS, and application layers.
- Familiarity with security compliance frameworks (ISO 27001, NIST CSF).
- Basic scripting ability for report automation (Python or PowerShell).

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Tenable.io or Qualys VMDR.
- Knowledge of cloud vulnerability management (AWS Inspector, Azure Defender).
- CompTIA Security+ or equivalent certification.
- Familiarity with penetration testing tools for validation scanning.
