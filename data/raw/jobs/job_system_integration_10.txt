Job Title: Enterprise Integration Consultant
Company: SynapseConnect

Job Description:
We are seeking an Enterprise Integration Consultant to help large organisations connect their legacy and modern systems. You will assess integration landscapes, design target architectures, and lead implementation of integration platforms.

Key Responsibilities:
- Assess existing system landscapes and identify integration gaps and redundancies.
- Design integration architectures using hub-and-spoke, point-to-point, and event-driven patterns.
- Lead implementation of integration platforms: MuleSoft, IBM App Connect, or Apache Camel.
- Define data governance standards for systems-of-record and master data management.
- Manage integration projects from requirements through to production go-live.
- Deliver technical workshops and documentation to client engineering teams.

Requirements:
- Strong understanding of enterprise integration patterns (EIP) and system integration approaches.
- Knowledge of JMS interfaces, RESTful APIs, and JSON serialisation for system communication.
- Experience with distributed system design including fault tolerance and data consistency.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with IoT integration patterns and message queue infrastructure for real-time data.
- Knowledge of traffic monitoring or logistics management system integration (TrafficFlow, LogisticsConnect).
- Familiarity with microservices and event-driven architecture modernisation patterns.
- MuleSoft Certified Integration Architect or IBM Integration Bus certification.
