Job Title: Flutter Mobile Developer (Entry Level)
Company: AppForge Studios

Job Description:
We are looking for a creative Flutter Developer to build cross-platform mobile applications for iOS and Android. You will work on the full app lifecycle from UI design to deployment on the app stores.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain high-performance mobile applications using Flutter and Dart.
- Implement Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for user management and data storage.
- Design clean, intuitive, and responsive user interfaces.
- Consume RESTful APIs and handle offline data caching.
- Debug and resolve performance issues and platform-specific bugs.
- Participate in code reviews and write unit tests.

Requirements:
- Familiarity with Flutter and Dart (coursework or personal projects accepted).
- Understanding of mobile UI/UX design principles.
- Knowledge of Git and version control.
- Ability to work in an agile team environment.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Push Notifications).
- Published an app on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- Understanding of state management (Provider, Riverpod, Bloc).
