Job Title: Data Operations Engineer
Company: CloudStream Data Solutions

Job Description:
We are looking for a Data Operations Engineer to manage our data infrastructure. You will ensure data pipelines are reliable, scalable, and efficient to power business intelligence dashboards.

Key Responsibilities:
- Monitor and maintain production data pipelines.
- Tune SQL queries for performance optimization.
- Implement data validation and reconciliation checks.
- Write scripts to automate data tasks (Python, Bash).
- Maintain documentation for data lineage and processes.

Requirements:
- Knowledge of relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL).
- Experience with SQL (joins, subqueries, window functions).
- Familiarity with Linux environment and command line.
- Strong attention to detail and ability to troubleshoot.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Apache Spark or Hadoop.
- Understanding of data warehousing concepts (Kimball/Inmon).
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI is a bonus.
