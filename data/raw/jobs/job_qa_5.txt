Job Title: Junior Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET)
Company: DevQuality Labs

Job Description:
We are looking for a Junior SDET to bridge the gap between development and quality assurance. You will build test infrastructure and write automated tests to ensure software quality.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and develop automated test frameworks from scratch.
- Write unit, integration, and end-to-end tests.
- Integrate test automation into CI/CD pipelines.
- Implement performance and load testing using JMeter.
- Collaborate with developers to understand features and dependencies.
- Maintain test environments and test data.

Requirements:
- Programming experience in Python, Java, or JavaScript.
- Understanding of object-oriented programming principles.
- Knowledge of Git and version control.
- Problem-solving and analytical thinking.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Selenium, JUnit, or TestNG.
- Understanding of BDD (Behavior Driven Development) tools like Cucumber.
- Experience with performance testing (JMeter).
