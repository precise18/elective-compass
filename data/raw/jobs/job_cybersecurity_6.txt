Job Title: Penetration Tester (Junior)
Company: RedTeam Africa

Job Description:
We are looking for a Junior Penetration Tester to join our offensive security team. You will perform authorised simulated attacks on client systems to identify vulnerabilities before malicious actors do.

Key Responsibilities:
- Conduct web application penetration tests (OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities).
- Perform network penetration testing including reconnaissance, scanning, and exploitation.
- Use industry-standard tools: Burp Suite, Nmap, Metasploit, and Wireshark.
- Write detailed penetration test reports with findings, risk ratings, and remediation steps.
- Assist clients in understanding and prioritising security vulnerabilities.
- Stay current with emerging attack techniques and CVEs.

Requirements:
- Understanding of networking protocols (TCP/IP, HTTP/S, DNS, FTP).
- Familiarity with Linux/Unix command line.
- Knowledge of OWASP Top 10 web vulnerabilities.
- Basic scripting skills (Python or Bash).

Nice-to-Have:
- Completion of TryHackMe or HackTheBox learning paths.
- CEH, CompTIA Security+, or OSCP certification (or in progress).
- Experience with Active Directory attacks (Kerberoasting, Pass-the-Hash).
- Knowledge of cloud security testing (AWS, Azure misconfigurations).
