The Role

You will lead the Cloud & DevOps team responsible for designing, building and operating Lesaka's cloud platforms and engineering capabilities.

You will oversee cloud operations, infrastructure engineering, DevOps pipelines and platform reliability while ensuring our environments remain secure, resilient and cost effective.

Leading a team of Cloud and DevOps Engineers, you will partner closely with Architecture, Security, Applications, ERP, CRM and Infrastructure teams to build platforms that enable reliable software delivery, support business growth and improve operational efficiency.

The role sits within the Operate and Optimise phases of the CIO value chain and plays a critical role in enabling secure, scalable and highly available technology services across the Merchant Division.

What You'll Own

    ️ Cloud Platform Operations & Reliability

    Operate and continuously improve enterprise cloud platforms across production, development and test environments.
    Ensure cloud services deliver high availability, resilience and operational stability.
    Lead incident response, problem management and root cause analysis to minimise service disruption.
    Maintain monitoring, logging and observability platforms that provide proactive operational insight.
    Lead disaster recovery and business continuity testing to ensure recovery objectives are consistently achieved.

    ️ Cloud Engineering & Infrastructure Optimisation

    Drive Infrastructure-as-Code adoption across all cloud environments using automation-first principles.
    Improve cloud performance, scalability and operational efficiency through continuous optimisation.
    Support migration of enterprise workloads into cloud-hosted environments.
    Implement cloud governance standards, resource tagging and cost optimisation practices.
    Reduce manual provisioning through infrastructure automation and standardisation.

DevOps Engineering & Delivery

    Lead the implementation and continuous improvement of enterprise CI/CD pipelines.
    Drive DevOps adoption across development teams through automation and engineering best practice.
    Implement automated testing, deployment, rollback and release management capabilities.
    Embed DevSecOps practices and security controls throughout the software delivery lifecycle.
    Improve deployment reliability while reducing release risk and increasing engineering productivity.

Connectivity, Security & Platform Integration

    Manage cloud networking, hybrid connectivity and secure integration between cloud and on-premise environments.
    Partner with Security teams to ensure cloud platforms comply with PCI DSS, SOX ITGC, POPIA and internal security standards.
    Support zero-trust networking, identity management and secure access controls.
    Work with Architecture and Applications teams to ensure cloud platforms support current and future business requirements.
    Monitor cloud platform performance and provide operational reporting to Infrastructure leadership.

People Leadership & Continuous Improvement

    Lead, coach and develop a high-performing Cloud & DevOps engineering team.
    Build technical capability across cloud engineering, automation and DevOps disciplines.
    Foster a culture of accountability, collaboration and continuous improvement.
    Reduce reliance on external service providers by strengthening internal engineering capability.
    Drive innovation and automation initiatives that improve operational performance and engineering maturity.
