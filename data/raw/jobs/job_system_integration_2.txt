Job Title: Middleware Developer
Company: Global Systems Connect

Job Description:
We are seeking a Middleware Developer to join our integration team. You will design, deploy, and maintain middleware solutions that connect legacy systems with modern cloud-based applications.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain integration components using enterprise service bus (ESB) tools.
- Write Java-based connectors to interface with external systems.
- Handle data mapping, transformation, and routing logic.
- Implement error handling and logging for integration flows.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand system requirements.
- Ensure high availability and fault tolerance of integration systems.

Requirements:
- Experience with Java and XML/JSON processing.
- Understanding of SOAP and REST web services.
- Knowledge of version control systems (Git).
- Excellent debugging and problem-solving skills.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with JMS and ActiveMQ.
- Understanding of distributed transaction management.
- Experience with containerization (Docker/Kubernetes).
