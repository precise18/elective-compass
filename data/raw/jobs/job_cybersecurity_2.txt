Job Title: Junior Security Analyst (SOC Analyst)
Company: SecureShield Networks

Job Description:
We are looking for a diligent Junior Security Analyst to join our Security Operations Center (SOC). You will monitor networks for threats, investigate security alerts, and help protect client infrastructure.

Key Responsibilities:
- Monitor security information and event management (SIEM) tools for alerts.
- Investigate potential security incidents and determine their severity.
- Analyze network traffic and system logs for malicious activities.
- Assist in vulnerability assessments and penetration testing.
- Develop and maintain security documentation and playbooks.
- Stay informed on the latest cybersecurity threats and trends.

Requirements:
- Understanding of TCP/IP, networking, and common attack vectors.
- Knowledge of Windows and Linux operating systems.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong attention to detail.

Nice-to-Have:
- CompTIA Security+ or similar certification.
- Experience with SIEM tools (Splunk, LogRhythm).
- Familiarity with penetration testing tools (Nmap, Burp Suite, Wireshark).
