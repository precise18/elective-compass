Job Title: Distributed Systems Engineer
Company: FaultTolerant Labs

Job Description:
We are looking for a Distributed Systems Engineer to build resilient, fault-tolerant systems that operate reliably across network partitions and partial failures. You will focus on the core engineering challenges of building services that work in the real world.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design distributed systems with explicit handling of partial failures and network partitions.
- Implement fault tolerance patterns: circuit breakers, bulkheads, timeouts, and fallbacks.
- Build distributed data consistency solutions using event sourcing and CQRS.
- Design and implement distributed monitoring and alerting using Prometheus and Grafana.
- Conduct chaos engineering experiments to validate system resilience.
- Create runbooks and post-mortem reports for distributed system incidents.

Requirements:
- Understanding of distributed systems fundamentals: CAP theorem, eventual consistency, consensus.
- Experience with message queue infrastructure (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or JMS).
- Knowledge of microservices architecture and inter-service communication.
- Proficiency in Java, Python, or Go for systems programming.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Kubernetes for container orchestration in distributed deployments.
- Knowledge of distributed tracing (Jaeger, Zipkin, OpenTelemetry).
- Familiarity with projects like HealthSafe or LogisticsConnect distributed system design patterns.
- Understanding of data replication and distributed database consistency.
