Job Title: NFT Platform Engineer
Company: ArtChain Studio

Job Description:
We are looking for an NFT Platform Engineer to build and maintain our NFT minting, trading, and display platform. You will work on smart contracts and the full-stack infrastructure that powers our creators' marketplace.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop ERC721 and ERC1155 NFT smart contracts with custom minting logic.
- Build metadata standards and integrate with IPFS for decentralised asset storage.
- Implement royalty standards (ERC2981) and allowlist minting mechanisms.
- Create NFT reveal, staking, and burn mechanics.
- Build frontend interfaces using React and ethers.js for minting and trading flows.
- Monitor contract events and build a backend indexer for NFT ownership data.

Requirements:
- Understanding of Solidity and ERC721/ERC1155 token standards.
- Familiarity with IPFS and decentralised storage (Pinata, NFT.Storage).
- Knowledge of OpenSea and NFT marketplace standards.
- Experience with JavaScript/TypeScript and frontend development.

Nice-to-Have:
- Knowledge of Solana NFT standards (Metaplex).
- Experience with Layer 2 networks (Polygon, Base, Arbitrum) for low-cost minting.
- Understanding of cross-chain NFT use cases and bridges.
- Experience with on-chain generative art or SVG rendering.
