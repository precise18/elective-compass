Job Title: Blockchain Backend Engineer
Company: TokenWorks Ltd

Job Description:
We are seeking a Blockchain Backend Engineer to develop and maintain server-side systems that interact with blockchain networks. You will build APIs, indexers, and event listeners that power our Web3 platform.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build Node.js or Python backend services that communicate with Ethereum and Solana nodes.
- Develop blockchain event listeners and indexers to track on-chain activity.
- Design and maintain REST and GraphQL APIs consumed by frontend dApps.
- Manage database storage for off-chain data (PostgreSQL, MongoDB).
- Implement wallet and transaction management logic.
- Integrate third-party blockchain services (Alchemy, Infura, Moralis).

Requirements:
- Strong backend development experience with Node.js or Python.
- Understanding of blockchain fundamentals: wallets, transactions, blocks, consensus.
- Familiarity with Ethereum JSON-RPC and provider libraries.
- Knowledge of relational and non-relational databases.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with The Graph protocol for indexing blockchain data.
- Knowledge of Solana Web3.js SDK.
- Understanding of BNB Chain and cross-chain bridging concepts.
- Experience with Docker and containerised deployments.
