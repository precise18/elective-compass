Job Title: Junior Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst
Company: CyberGuard Defense

Job Description:
We are seeking a motivated Junior SOC Analyst to join our 24/7 security operations team. You will monitor security alerts, investigate potential threats, and help protect client infrastructure from cyber attacks.

Key Responsibilities:
- Monitor SIEM platforms (Splunk, IBM QRadar) for security alerts.
- Analyze network traffic and system logs to identify suspicious activity.
- Triage and escalate security incidents according to playbooks.
- Assist in vulnerability scanning and penetration testing activities.
- Document incident response procedures and create reports.
- Stay current on emerging cyber threats and attack techniques.

Requirements:
- Understanding of TCP/IP networking and OSI model.
- Knowledge of Windows and Linux operating systems.
- Familiarity with common attack vectors (phishing, malware, DDoS).
- Excellent analytical and investigative skills.
- Strong attention to detail and documentation.

Nice-to-Have:
- CompTIA Security+ or CySA+ certification.
- Experience with Wireshark or tcpdump.
- Basic scripting skills (Python, Bash).
