Job Title: Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)
Company: UpTime Systems

Job Description:
We are looking for a Site Reliability Engineer to ensure the reliability, performance, and scalability of our cloud-hosted services. You will apply software engineering principles to infrastructure and operations problems.

Key Responsibilities:
- Define and track Service Level Objectives (SLOs) and error budgets for critical services.
- Build and maintain observability stacks using Prometheus, Grafana, and distributed tracing.
- Automate operational toil with Python and Bash scripts.
- Design and test disaster recovery and failover procedures.
- Conduct blameless post-mortems and implement preventative measures.
- Optimise cloud resource usage and costs across AWS services.

Requirements:
- Strong Linux/Unix systems knowledge and shell scripting.
- Experience with monitoring and alerting tools.
- Understanding of cloud computing concepts and distributed systems.
- Programming proficiency in Python or Go.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration at scale.
- Knowledge of chaos engineering principles (AWS Fault Injection Simulator).
- Familiarity with AWS cost management tools (Cost Explorer, Savings Plans).
- Understanding of database reliability (RDS Multi-AZ, Aurora Global Clusters).
