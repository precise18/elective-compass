Job Title: DeFi Protocol Developer
Company: OpenYield Finance

Job Description:
We are hiring a DeFi Protocol Developer to design and implement decentralised finance smart contracts. You will contribute to building lending, staking, and liquidity pool contracts deployed on EVM-compatible networks.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and implement DeFi smart contracts including AMMs, lending pools, and yield farms.
- Write comprehensive unit and integration tests using Hardhat and Foundry.
- Implement upgradeable contract patterns (Transparent Proxy, UUPS).
- Optimise gas consumption in contract logic and data storage.
- Analyse and implement tokenomics models for governance and utility tokens.
- Review and integrate third-party DeFi protocol interfaces (Chainlink, Uniswap V3).

Requirements:
- Proficiency in Solidity smart contract development.
- Understanding of DeFi primitives: AMMs, liquidity pools, staking, flash loans.
- Knowledge of ERC20, ERC721, ERC1155 token standards.
- Strong understanding of the Ethereum Virtual Machine and gas optimisation.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Vyper as an alternative to Solidity.
- Familiarity with formal verification tools.
- Understanding of MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) and its implications.
- Experience with cross-chain DeFi using bridges or messaging protocols.
