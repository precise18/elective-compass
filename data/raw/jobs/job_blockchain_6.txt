Job Title: Smart Contract Auditor
Company: ChainSec Labs

Job Description:
We are seeking a detail-oriented Smart Contract Auditor to review and audit Solidity and Vyper smart contracts for security vulnerabilities. You will help our clients deploy secure decentralised applications on Ethereum and EVM-compatible chains.

Key Responsibilities:
- Perform manual and automated security audits of Solidity/Vyper smart contracts.
- Identify vulnerabilities such as reentrancy, integer overflow, and access control flaws.
- Write detailed audit reports with severity ratings and remediation guidance.
- Use tools like Slither, Mythril, and Echidna for static and dynamic analysis.
- Collaborate with development teams to implement security fixes.
- Stay current with known attack vectors (e.g., flash loan attacks, front-running).

Requirements:
- Strong understanding of Solidity or Vyper smart contract development.
- Knowledge of common smart contract vulnerabilities (OWASP Smart Contract Top 10).
- Familiarity with the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM).
- Experience using blockchain testing frameworks (Hardhat, Foundry, Brownie).

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with DeFi protocols (Uniswap, Aave, Compound).
- Understanding of cryptographic primitives (hashing, signatures, Merkle trees).
- Contributions to open-source security research or bug bounty programs.
