Job Title: Security Operations Centre (SOC) Analyst
Company: CyberWatch Solutions

Job Description:
We are hiring a SOC Analyst to monitor, detect, and respond to cybersecurity threats in real time. You will be the first line of defence, triaging alerts and escalating incidents within our 24/7 operations centre.

Key Responsibilities:
- Monitor security alerts from SIEM platforms (Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, QRadar).
- Triage and investigate security events to determine severity and impact.
- Respond to incidents following established playbooks and escalation procedures.
- Analyse logs from firewalls, endpoints, and cloud platforms for indicators of compromise.
- Produce incident reports and document findings in ticketing systems.
- Tune detection rules to reduce false positives and improve alert fidelity.

Requirements:
- Understanding of common attack techniques (phishing, malware, lateral movement).
- Familiarity with SIEM tools and log analysis.
- Knowledge of network security fundamentals and common protocols.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, time-sensitive environment.

Nice-to-Have:
- TryHackMe SOC Level 1 path or equivalent training.
- CompTIA Security+ or CySA+ certification.
- Experience with endpoint detection and response (EDR) tools.
- Knowledge of MITRE ATT&CK framework for threat classification.
