Job Title: IT Security Intern (Penetration Testing)
Company: BlackBox Security

Job Description:
We are looking for a motivated Penetration Testing intern to join our ethical hacking team. You will learn to identify vulnerabilities in web applications, APIs, and network infrastructure.

Key Responsibilities:
- Assist senior penetration testers in conducting assessments.
- Use industry-standard tools (Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP, Metasploit) to identify security flaws.
- Document findings and create detailed vulnerability reports.
- Perform social engineering tests (phishing simulations).
- Research emerging threats and exploit techniques.

Requirements:
- Passion for cybersecurity and ethical hacking.
- Basic knowledge of networking (TCP/IP, firewalls).
- Understanding of web application vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10).
- Strong verbal and written communication skills.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Kali Linux and open-source security tools.
- Participation in CTF (Capture The Flag) competitions.
- Basic scripting knowledge (Python, Bash, or PowerShell).
