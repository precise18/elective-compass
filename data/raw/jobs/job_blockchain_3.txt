Job Title: Smart Contract Developer (Junior)
Company: ChainTech Labs

Job Description:
We are looking for a passionate Smart Contract Developer to join our Web3 team. You will design, implement, and audit smart contracts using Solidity and Vyper for DeFi and NFT applications.

Key Responsibilities:
- Write secure and gas-efficient smart contracts for Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM).
- Develop and test smart contracts using Hardhat and Foundry.
- Build full-stack dApps integrating smart contracts with React and Web3.js.
- Perform code audits to identify security vulnerabilities (re-entrancy, overflow).
- Deploy contracts to testnets and mainnet.
- Stay updated on the latest EIPs (Ethereum Improvement Proposals) and industry standards.

Requirements:
- Strong understanding of blockchain fundamentals and cryptography.
- Proficiency in Solidity (or Vyper) and familiarity with development frameworks.
- Understanding of the Ethereum ecosystem (ERC-20, ERC-721, ERC-1155).
- Knowledge of OpenZeppelin contracts and best practices.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with DEXs (Uniswap, PancakeSwap) or DeFi protocols.
- Understanding of IPFS or other decentralized storage.
- Contributions to open-source blockchain projects.
