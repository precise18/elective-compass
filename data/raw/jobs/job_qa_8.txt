Job Title: QA Lead / Senior QA Engineer
Company: QualityFirst Tech

Job Description:
We are looking for a QA Lead to own the quality strategy for multiple product teams. You will mentor junior QA engineers, define testing standards, and ensure quality is embedded throughout the software development lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities:
- Define and maintain the quality assurance strategy, processes, and standards across teams.
- Lead test planning, test design, and test execution for major product releases.
- Mentor and grow junior QA engineers in both manual and automated testing disciplines.
- Drive adoption of the Test Automation Pyramid across the engineering organisation.
- Manage defect triage, prioritisation, and reporting to stakeholders.
- Champion exploratory testing, risk-based testing, and shift-left quality practices.

Requirements:
- Strong experience in both manual and automated testing methodologies.
- Deep understanding of software testing principles, test design techniques, and test coverage metrics.
- Experience managing test cycles and working with project management tools.
- Excellent communication and leadership skills.

Nice-to-Have:
- ISTQB Advanced Level Test Manager certification.
- Experience with BDD frameworks (Cucumber, Behave).
- Knowledge of performance and security testing as part of a holistic QA strategy.
- Familiarity with agile and DevOps quality practices.
