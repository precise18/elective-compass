Job Title: Android Developer (Kotlin)
Company: NativeMobile SA

Job Description:
We are seeking an Android Developer to build and maintain high-quality native Android applications using Kotlin. You will work on the full app lifecycle, from architecture design to Play Store deployment.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop native Android apps using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.
- Implement modern Android architecture patterns (MVVM, Clean Architecture).
- Integrate REST APIs using Retrofit and manage local data with Room database.
- Implement Firebase services: Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, and Crashlytics.
- Write unit tests and UI tests using JUnit and Espresso.
- Publish and maintain app releases on the Google Play Store.

Requirements:
- Understanding of Kotlin programming fundamentals.
- Knowledge of Android SDK and core Jetpack libraries (ViewModel, LiveData, Navigation).
- Familiarity with mobile UI/UX design principles and Material Design.
- Experience with version control (Git).

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Flutter for cross-platform development alongside native.
- Knowledge of Coroutines and Flow for asynchronous programming.
- Familiarity with CI/CD for mobile (Fastlane, Firebase App Distribution).
- Published app on the Google Play Store.
