Job Title: iOS/Android App Developer (Entry Level)
Company: MobileFirst Technologies

Job Description:
We are looking for an Entry Level Mobile App Developer to join our team. You will develop, test, and maintain mobile applications for iOS and Android platforms.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop high-performance mobile applications using Flutter or native technologies.
- Implement UI/UX designs with clean, maintainable code.
- Integrate with RESTful APIs and JSON data.
- Write unit tests and debug application issues.
- Optimize application performance for Android and iOS.
- Submit applications to Google Play and Apple App Store.

Requirements:
- Programming experience (Java, Kotlin, Swift, or Dart).
- Understanding of mobile app lifecycle and development tools.
- Knowledge of version control systems (Git).
- Strong communication and teamwork skills.

Nice-to-Have:
- Experience with Flutter or React Native.
- Knowledge of Firebase or AWS Amplify.
- Previous mobile app development coursework or personal project.
